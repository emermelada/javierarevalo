## Problem

A working Steadicam operator needed a professional web presence — fast, on a
small budget, and good enough to send to producers. Traditional agency
timelines and pricing didn't fit; neither did a template that looked like a
template.

## Solution

I use large language models as a delivery multiplier, with myself as project
lead and QA rather than typist:

- Architected the content structure and defined the brief, then ran an
  AI-assisted UI prototyping workflow — directing and iterating with the model.
- Audited and refactored the generated code to a production standard: bugs,
  accessibility, semantics and structure, before anything shipped.
- Managed the GitHub Pages deployment and configured custom-domain routing
  with SSL/TLS via Cloudflare.

## Result

A complete, custom site delivered end to end in under 48 hours, live at
[joserodriguez.mov](https://joserodriguez.mov). The client edits nothing and
maintains nothing — hosting is free, TLS is automatic, and the delivery
process is repeatable for the next client.
